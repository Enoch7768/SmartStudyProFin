# SmartStudyPro Accessibility Statement

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective date:** 31 October 2026

SmartStudyPro aims to make learning and account services usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies, keyboard navigation, zoom, captions or alternative input methods.

## Our approach

We aim to use semantic page structure, descriptive headings, labels and link text; keep interactive controls keyboard accessible and provide visible focus states; give form fields clear labels and useful validation messages; provide text alternatives for meaningful images and accessible names for icon-only controls; respect reduced-motion preferences where animations are not essential; maintain readable contrast, responsive layouts and usable zoom; avoid relying on colour alone to communicate important status; and make learning materials available in accessible formats where reasonably practicable.

## Supported environments

The website is designed for current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on desktop and mobile, with keyboard navigation and commonly used screen readers. Actual support can vary with browser, assistive technology, third-party content, uploaded course files and the accessibility of materials supplied by content authors.

## Known limitations

Some legacy pages, third-party payment screens, embedded media, uploaded PDFs, images, quizzes and administrator-authored content may not fully meet accessibility expectations. Automated checks cannot establish full WCAG conformance. We do not claim certification or complete conformance at this time.

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

Email smartstudypro36@gmail.com with the page or feature, the assistive technology/browser if known, and what you were trying to do. You may also call +256 704 416250. Please do not include passwords or sensitive student records in a report.

We will review reported barriers and prioritise practical fixes. This statement will be updated as accessibility testing and remediation progress.
