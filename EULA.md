# SmartStudyPro Website Software End-User Licence Agreement

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective date:** 31 October 2026  
**Status:** Client execution draft — not effective until completed and signed by both parties.

This agreement records the limited right to use the SmartStudyPro website software. It is between the developer who created and supplies the software (the Developer or Licensor) and the client identified in the signed project/development agreement (the Client or Licensee).

The legal names, service addresses, notice emails, licensed domain, support scope, fees and signatures must be confirmed in the project agreement. This online copy is a reference copy and does not replace the signed agreement.

## 1. Ownership and reservation of rights

The Developer retains all right, title, interest and intellectual-property rights in the original software and developer-created materials, including source code, architecture, database structure, custom modules, workflows, integrations, design implementation, documentation, updates and derivative works. All rights not expressly licensed to the Client are reserved by the Developer.

The Client does not purchase or acquire ownership of the software, source code, underlying platform, reusable components, development methods or intellectual property merely by paying development, setup, hosting, maintenance or support fees.

Client-provided content, names, logos, trademarks, student records and other materials remain owned by the Client or their respective rights holders. The Client grants the Developer permission to use those materials only as reasonably necessary to build, maintain, support and deliver the contracted website.

Third-party software and assets are excluded from the Developer's ownership claim and remain subject to their own licences and notices.

## 2. Licence granted to the Client

Subject to the signed project agreement and payment of applicable fees, the Developer grants the Client a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable licence to operate the deployed SmartStudyPro website for the Client's own education and business activities at the domain(s) identified in the project agreement.

The licence does not include a right to distribute the source code, provide the software as a service to unrelated third parties, resell or sublicense the software, remove ownership notices, or deploy additional copies or domains unless the Developer agrees in writing.

The Client may use the website's normal administrative features to manage its own content, users, courses, products, bookings, orders and learning records. This permission does not transfer ownership of the software that provides those features.

## 3. Restrictions

The Client must not, and must not permit another person to:

- Copy, publish, sell, rent, lease, sublicense, distribute or commercially exploit the software or source code except as expressly permitted in writing.
- Remove, obscure or alter copyright, proprietary or attribution notices.
- Reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the software except to the extent such restriction is prohibited by mandatory law.
- Extract or reuse proprietary modules, source code, design systems or developer-created components in another product or website.
- Circumvent licence, access, payment, security or technical restrictions.
- Use the website for unlawful activity, infringement, fraud, spam, malware distribution or unauthorised data collection.
- Give hosting, development or other third parties access to source code except where authorised by the Developer and bound by confidentiality and equivalent restrictions.

## 4. Client responsibilities

The Client is responsible for the legality, accuracy and rights clearance of content it uploads or publishes; its user accounts and administrator access; the instructions it gives to staff; its lawful collection and use of personal data; and its compliance with education, consumer, tax, privacy and other rules applicable to its operations.

The Client must use strong unique credentials, restrict administrator access, promptly remove access for former staff, keep contact and payment-provider details current, and promptly notify the Developer of suspected compromise.

Unless expressly included in the signed project agreement, hosting, domain registration, third-party subscriptions, payment-provider fees, email delivery, content entry, ongoing moderation, legal advice and continuous support are not included in the software licence.

## 5. Hosting, maintenance and updates

The software is supplied for deployment in the agreed hosting environment. The Developer is not responsible for outages, changes, security incidents or service withdrawal caused by hosting providers, domain registrars, payment gateways, AI providers, email services or other third parties outside the Developer's reasonable control.

Maintenance, support response times, backups, recovery objectives, update entitlement and additional domains must be defined in the signed project agreement. No perpetual maintenance or hosting service is implied by this licence.

## 6. Personal data and confidentiality

Each party must comply with applicable data-protection law. The parties must separately document their controller/processor roles, permitted processing, security measures, subprocessors, breach assistance, retention and deletion arrangements where the Developer processes personal data on the Client's behalf.

The Client remains responsible for determining the lawful purposes for which student, parent, staff, customer and visitor data is collected. The Developer may access such data only as authorised and necessary to deliver agreed services.

## 7. Fees, suspension and termination

Licence fees, payment milestones, renewal charges and any suspension rights are governed by the signed project agreement. If the Client materially breaches this agreement and does not remedy the breach within a reasonable written notice period, the Developer may suspend or terminate the licence to the extent permitted by law and the project agreement.

On termination, the Client must stop using the licensed software and return or securely delete Developer confidential materials and unauthorised copies. The parties must agree a lawful export and handover of Client content and personal data before access is disabled. Termination does not transfer software ownership to the Client.

Sections concerning ownership, restrictions, confidentiality, liability, accrued payment obligations and dispute resolution survive termination to the extent necessary to give them effect.

## 8. Warranty and liability

The project agreement must state the agreed acceptance criteria, defect warranty period and support obligations. Except for express written warranties and non-excludable rights under applicable law, the software is provided on an as-delivered basis. The Developer does not warrant uninterrupted operation or that third-party services will remain available or unchanged.

Neither party excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Any other limitation of liability, indemnity or allocation of risk must be expressly completed in the signed project agreement and reviewed by qualified counsel.

## 9. Governing law and disputes

The governing law, courts or agreed dispute-resolution process must be stated in the signed project agreement. If the parties intend Ugandan law to govern, they should expressly confirm that choice in the signed agreement and obtain local legal review.

## 10. Entire agreement and changes

This licence and the signed project agreement form the agreement between the parties concerning use of the software. If they conflict, the signed project agreement controls only where it expressly identifies the provision being varied. Any change or additional licence must be in writing and signed by both parties.
