## 2. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, the platform may process:

- Account details: name, email address, password hash, account status and authentication/session information.
- Profile details: phone number, location, biography, avatar and theme preference.
- Learning records: course enrolments, lessons accessed, progress positions, bookmarks, quiz answers, scores, assessment attempts, achievements and certificates.
- Commerce records: cart and order details, item descriptions, prices, currency, payment status, payment-provider references, refunds or fulfilment information where supported.
- Support and booking information: information you send through contact, booking or support channels.
- AI assistant information: prompts, conversation history and relevant course excerpts used to prepare a response.
- Security and technical information: request metadata, rate-limit records, hashed network identifiers, browser/session data and diagnostic events necessary to protect and operate the service.
- Legal records: the versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy accepted, acceptance timestamps and hashed request metadata used to evidence acceptance.

Do not send passwords, card security codes or unnecessary sensitive information in messages or AI prompts.

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide courses, progress tracking, assessments and certificates; process orders and payments; deliver downloads; respond to requests; provide AI-assisted learning where enabled; prevent fraud and abuse; maintain backups and service reliability; meet legal obligations; and resolve disputes.

We aim to collect only information that is relevant to these purposes.
