# SmartStudyPro Cookie and Local Storage Notice

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective date:** 31 October 2026

SmartStudyPro uses a small number of browser technologies needed to operate the website and remember your display preference.

## Technologies used by the website

- Authentication session cookie: supports signed-in sessions and security controls. It is an essential cookie and is not used for advertising.
- Theme preference in local storage: remembers whether you selected light or dark appearance. This value stays in your browser and is not an authentication credential.

The application does not intentionally use advertising cookies or cross-site behavioural advertising trackers. If analytics, embedded media, chat widgets or additional tracking technologies are introduced, this Notice must be updated and any required consent must be obtained before those technologies are activated.

## Third-party services

When you leave SmartStudyPro to sign in with or pay through a third-party provider, that provider may use its own cookies and similar technologies under its own privacy and cookie notices. We do not control those technologies.

## Managing browser storage

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing the session cookie signs you out. Clearing the theme preference resets the website's appearance preference. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in and account features from working.
