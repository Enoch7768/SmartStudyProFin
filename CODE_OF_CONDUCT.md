# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The SmartStudyPro project is committed to a respectful, safe and constructive environment for maintainers, contributors, learners and community members.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, considerate and professional.
- Give feedback about work and ideas, not personal characteristics.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and intellectual-property rights.
- Share security issues responsibly and avoid publishing personal data.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, impersonation, malicious disruption, sexual harassment, publication of private information and deliberate infringement or misuse of project or client materials are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to smartstudypro36@gmail.com. Include relevant context and links, but do not publish private student, customer or security information. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practicable. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or take other proportionate action where necessary to protect people and the project.
